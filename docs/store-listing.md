## Detailed description

Albert Planner turns your NYU Albert shopping cart into a weekly calendar you can actually plan with.

NEW IN 1.3: PLAN WITH GOALS
• Describe what you want — "no Friday classes, nothing before 10, at most 16 credits" — and Chrome's built-in AI proposes matching rules. You see each change against your current rules, keep the ones you want, and nothing applies until you confirm.
• Or set rules yourself: credit limit, earliest start, days off, and must / maybe / skip for each section.
• Get up to three schedule options from a complete search over every section in your cart, sorted by a stated order. Each option shows what differs from the first one, and every course left out comes with a reason ("Overlaps CSCI-UA 201 on Mon", "Meeting times are unconfirmed").
• Apply an option to your planner in one click. If your cart or rules changed in the meantime, it asks you to regenerate instead of applying stale results.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Goal text is read on your device by Chrome's built-in model. It isn't sent to us or anyone else, and it isn't saved.
• The model download, if your Chrome needs it, starts only when you click.
• No accounts and no analytics. A few usage counts stay on your device and are shared only if you choose to include them in feedback.

EVERYTHING ELSE
• Import courses from the Shopping Cart or enrollment summary
• Priority buckets, a five-day calendar, conflict and TBA detection
• Credit totals and weekly hours
• Rate My Professors ratings for matched instructors
• Export your week as a PNG

On-device AI needs a recent desktop Chrome (138+) on hardware that supports Gemini Nano. Everything except the goal box works without it.

Albert Planner is an independent open-source project. NYU and Rate My Professors do not sponsor or endorse it.
